Every chapter closes with one artifact. Chapter 1 closes with the Storybook.

It is the canonical brand document—worldview, audience, differentiation, language, and visual identity gathered into a single place you can stand behind for years.

## Why it exists

The practical answer is that the work of Chapter 1 is otherwise scattered across a messaging document, an audience profile, a strategy, and a folder of design files, which means that in eighteen months nobody can find the whole picture and a new marketing hire reconstructs it from the website. The Storybook is the consolidation that prevents that.

The real answer is that a company needs one document it can hand to someone and say: this is who we are. Not the pitch, not the deck, not the about page. The account that the pitch and the deck and the about page are all derived from.

## What is in it

Your worldview—what you believe about your category and why the company exists at all. The audience, described the way § 1.1 described them. The differentiation, in the single sentence that survived the pressure test. The language: the words you use, the words you avoid, the narratives you return to. And the visual system, so that the way the brand looks and the way it sounds are visibly the same claim.

## What it looks like

The form varies. Some partners want a printed artifact, physically well made, that sits on a desk and gets handed to new executives. Others want a living page their team can reference and search. Both are the Storybook. The name and the definition of complete stay constant, and the medium follows how your company actually works.

We will ask you which you want, and if you are unsure, we default to whichever one your team is more likely to actually open.

## When it is done

When it exists and you have approved it, Chapter 1 is closed. That is the whole definition, and we keep it strict on purpose. “Done” is the hardest word in this business, and an artifact is what gives it a meaning you can hold.

Closing the chapter does not mean the message is frozen. It gets revisited quarterly or annually through a [messaging refresh](/story/message/messaging-refresh), and the Storybook is updated with it. But the foundation is settled, and Chapter 2 can be built on top of something that is not still moving.
