Every Snapmarket partnership is a manuscript we write with you over years. Not a campaign, not a retainer of scattered deliverables—a body of work that compounds, with chapters, a shape, and a clear sense of where you are in it at any given moment.

The manuscript has three chapters and one layer running underneath. Story is the message. System is the engine. Send is the disciplined act of putting work into the world on a schedule. Scale is the measurement, and it starts on the first day rather than arriving at month eighteen.

This site exists so you never have to wonder what happens next. It covers what each chapter contains, what we will ask of you, what you will hold at the end of it, and how to reach the accounts and platforms your work lives in. It is part philosophy and part instruction manual, which is roughly the ratio of the work itself.

## How to read this site

Each chapter has an overview page that explains what the chapter is for and when it typically happens, then a page for each section inside it, then a page for each piece of work that section can produce. Read the overview if you want the shape. Read the piece pages when a specific piece is on your calendar and you want to know what your week looks like.

Every piece page answers the same four questions in the same order. What this is. What we need from you. What you will have when it is done. How long it usually takes. We have deliberately left out our internal production detail, because knowing how a kitchen sequences its stations does not make the meal better. What you should be able to see is the whole floor plan.

## Who to ask

Anything urgent goes to your account lead directly. Anything that is a request for work, a question about a deliverable, or a change of scope is best sent to [support@snapmarket.co](mailto:support@snapmarket.co), which puts it in front of the whole team and gives it a place in the queue rather than living in one person's inbox.

<Note>
  This site describes how we work in general. Your partnership has its own scope, cadence, and priorities, all of which are set in your proposal and revisited in the monthly audit. Where the two differ, your agreement governs.
</Note>
