Every Snapmarket partnership becomes what we call a manuscript that we write with you over years. Not a campaign, not a retainer of scattered deliverables—a body of work that compounds, with chapters, a shape, and a clear sense of where you are in it at any given moment.

The manuscript has three chapters and one layer running underneath.

1. Story is the message and visual identity.
2. System is the technical infrastructure.
3. Send is the disciplined act of putting work into the world on a schedule, usually in the form of a podcast, which we’ll explain.
4. Scale is the measurement, and it starts on the first day your website is live.

This site exists so you never have to wonder what happens next. It covers what each chapter contains, what we will ask of you, what you will hold at the end of it, and how to reach the accounts and platforms your work lives in. It is part philosophy and part instruction manual, which is roughly the ratio of the work itself.

## How to read this site

Each chapter has an overview page that explains what the chapter is for and when it typically happens, then a page for each section inside it, then a page for each piece of work that section can produce. Read the overview if you want the shape. Read the sub-pages about individual units of work called “pieces” when a specific piece is on your calendar and you want to know what your week looks like.

Every piece page answers the same four questions in the same order.

1. What this is.
2. What we need from you.
3. What you will have when it is done.
4. How long it usually takes.

## Who to ask

If you ever have any questions, please email [support@snapmarket.co](mailto:support@snapmarket.co), which puts it in front of the whole team so they can get to work efficiently.
