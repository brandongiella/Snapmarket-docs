## What this is

The standing monthly reporting cadence. We pull the data, interpret it, and tell you what it means. Every partner, every month, without being asked.

The interpretation is the piece that matters. A dashboard link is not a report. Anyone can send you a number that went up, and a number that went up with no account of why is indistinguishable from luck.

## How this differs from the audit

Worth being clear, because you receive both.

Performance reporting is the standing account of what happened—traffic, engagement, conversions, channel performance, movement against the prior period. It is regular, comparable month to month, and comparatively short.

The [monthly audit](/scale/audit) is the deeper strategic review. It reads every chapter of your manuscript, assigns a status to each, measures against your standing thesis, and names what the next period is betting on. It is an editor's letter rather than a report.

One tells you what the instruments read. The other tells you what we think it means and what we propose to do about it.

## What you will get

A report you can forward to your board without translating it first. Prose alongside the numbers, the trend rather than the isolated figure, and an explicit note wherever a number is not trustworthy yet—a tracking gap, a partial month, a change that broke comparability.

During the [baseline period](/scale/baseline), reporting covers instrumentation health and qualitative observation only, and makes no quantitative claims at all. We say so on the page rather than filling the space with numbers that cannot yet mean anything.

## What we need from you

Almost nothing, which is the point. It arrives whether or not you ask.

The one thing worth doing is telling us when something outside marketing moves—a pricing change, a new salesperson, a large customer signed through a personal relationship. Context we do not have is the most common reason a report misreads a month.

## What this is not

Not the initial setup. Not the [monthly audit](/scale/audit). Not a live dashboard you are expected to check daily, though you have access to one if you want it.
